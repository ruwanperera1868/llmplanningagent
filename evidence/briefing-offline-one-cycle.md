## Summary

## Key findings

- This survey gives a systematic view of planning in agents built on large language models. (Huang et al., 2024)
- It proposes a unified framework covering profiling, memory, planning and action modules. (Wang et al., 2023)
- An evaluation shows that observation, planning and reflection each contribute to believable individual and emergent social behaviour. (Park et al., 2023)
- This survey traces the concept of agents from philosophy to artificial intelligence and argues that large language models are suitable foundations for general agents. (Xi et al., 2023)
- Reasoning helps the model plan and update its plan, while actions let it gather external information. (Yao et al., 2022)
- After each trial the agent reflects verbally on task feedback and stores the reflection in an episodic memory buffer. (Shinn et al., 2023)

## Conflicting or inconclusive evidence

- LLM+P: Empowering Large Language Models with Optimal Planning Proficiency: LLM+P translates a natural-language planning problem into the Planning Domain Definition Language, calls a classical planner to find an optimal plan, and translates the plan back into natural language.
- Plan-and-Solve Prompting: Improving Zero-Shot Chain-of-Thought Reasoning by Large Language Models: Plan-and-Solve prompting first asks the model to devise a plan that divides the task into subtasks, then to carry out the subtasks according to the plan.
