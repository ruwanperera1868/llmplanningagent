# Research briefing

**Question:** Does generative AI improve university students' academic performance?

**Evidence status:** Judged sufficient and balanced after 1 search cycle(s).

_Generated by: llm reporting; appraisals by llm. This briefing supports, and does not replace, the researcher's own reading._

## Summary

The evidence from the reviewed papers is mixed, with some supporting the positive impact of generative AI on academic performance and others opposing it, particularly in terms of digital fatigue and negative impacts on learning outcomes.

## Key findings

- There is some support for the positive impact of generative AI on academic performance, particularly in enhancing learning outcomes and improving student engagement. (Tian, 2026; Hon, 2025; Yang, 2026; McKenzie and Day, 2025; George, 2025; Abola, 2026; Pallant et al., 2025; Chan et al., 2024; Bai and Wang, 2025; Gunsaldi et al., 2025; Weng et al., 2024)
- Some studies indicate that generative AI can negatively impact academic performance, particularly through digital fatigue and negative learning outcomes. (Yang, 2026; Bai and Wang, 2025; Gunsaldi et al., 2025; Ju, 2023; Weng et al., 2024)

## Conflicting or inconclusive evidence

- The review by doi:10.1177/00472395251400089 reports mixed evidence on the impact of generative AI on learning outcomes, while other papers (doi:10.21203/rs.3.rs-9819153/v1, doi:10.47408/jldhe.vi41.1931, doi:10.1080/03075079.2025.2487570) support a positive impact.
- doi:10.2139/ssrn.7358579 and doi:10.1038/s41598-025-08697-6 both suggest negative impacts, but doi:10.55549/jeseh.840 and doi:10.14742/ajet.9540 also support positive impacts.

## Limitations

- Most of the papers do not specify the exact impact of generative AI on academic performance, focusing more on the mechanisms or broader effects.
- Longitudinal and large-scale studies are identified as gaps in the literature by doi:10.1177/00472395251400089.

## Possible research opportunities (within retrieved evidence only)

- There is a need for more longitudinal and large-scale studies to directly measure the impact of generative AI on academic performance.
- Further research is needed to understand the specific mechanisms through which generative AI influences learning outcomes and academic performance.
